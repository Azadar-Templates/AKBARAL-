Withdraw — verified balance only

          

            No payout account is required at setup. Verified earnings accumulate internally while payout slots
            remain unconfigured. When you are ready to withdraw, add or select a payout destination here — only a
            verified slot can receive funds. Every withdrawal needs owner approval and a provider
            settlement reference. The requested amount is atomically reserved (deducted/frozen) on
            approval to prevent double withdrawal; if the provider reports failure the reservation is safely restored.
          

          

            Destination storage: only masked account (e.g. ****1234) or provider reference (e.g. Stripe
            ba_… / Payoneer account id) — raw banking credentials are never stored. Withdrawals of
            unverified, expected, contracted or synthetic revenue are refused; only received + verifier
            counts.

Withdrawals — lifecycle

          
REQUESTED (pending_approval) → VERIFYING (destination checks) → APPROVED (atomically debited payout:reserve) → PROCESSING (sent) → PAID (settled) or FAILED (refund payout:refund + restore). All transitions audited and idempotent.

          


          
Wallets & ledger — wallet architecture

          
Per-agent wallets (4000+), central Mission Treasury, and reinvestment reserve. Separate from public-platform customer funds (different database, different auth). Balances never go negative; ledger is hash-chained (seq + prev_hash) and idempotent.
